Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Tijarah ("we", "us") is a Pakistan-based B2B & B2C marketplace currently in pre-launch. This policy explains what we collect through the coming-soon page at tijarah.pk and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Email address — when you join the launch waitlist.
  • Technical metadata — subscription timestamp and the page you subscribed from.

We do not collect names, payment data, or use advertising trackers on this page.

How we use it

  • Send a confirmation/welcome email when you subscribe.
  • Send occasional product updates and the launch announcement.

We do not sell, rent, or share your email with advertisers.

Processors

  • Mailjet (Sinch) — delivers our emails.
  • Amazon Web Services — stores the waitlist (DynamoDB, us-east-1).
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this static page.

Your choices

Every email includes a one-click Unsubscribe link that removes you immediately. You can also email support@tijarah.pk to access, correct, or delete your data.

Retention

Waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe or the waitlist is retired, whichever comes first.
